HOW TO PLAY

Five magical trials. One hidden treasure.

Follow the map. Each location has a different game. Complete a trial to earn coins, stars and a Starfire shard. When you collect all five shards, the Ancient Vault opens.

Important: In Firefly Grove, tap two cards and find identical symbols. In the Ancient Vault, follow the three numbered clues in order.
